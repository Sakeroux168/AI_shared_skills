## Rights boundary

The upstream disclaimer states that community-sourced prompts and example images remain associated
with their original sources and are not guaranteed for commercial use. This repository therefore
does not declare the full case or image collection uniformly MIT-licensed. Case attribution and
source/platform terms must be retained, and commercial use requires separate rights review.

## Future upstream commits

This acceptance evidence covers only the locked commit above. A changed upstream commit
automatically sets each affected Skill to experimental and adds a pending trust_review record.
After the required automated and manual acceptance is recorded, a separate reviewed commit may
explicitly restore trusted and remove that pending record.
